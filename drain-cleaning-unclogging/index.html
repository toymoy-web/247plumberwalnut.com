A slow kitchen sink, a gurgling shower drain, or a main line backing up into a tub: drain clogs in Walnut, CA usually start as a slow drain and get worse over days or weeks, not all at once. We clear the clog the same visit in most cases, and check why it happened before we leave, since a drain that clogs again in a month usually means the first fix only addressed the symptom.

Most single-fixture clogs (a sink, a tub, a single toilet) come from grease, soap scum, hair, or small debris caught close to the fixture. Those usually clear with a standard drain snake (cable auger) run through the line. A clog that returns repeatedly is different. So is a clog that affects more than one fixture at once, or one that sits in the main line itself. Those usually point to something further down the pipe: root intrusion from mature landscaping, a partial line collapse, or years of grease buildup that a snake can push through but not remove. For those, hydro jetting clears the full diameter of the pipe with high-pressure water, rather than just punching a hole through the blockage.

Walnut's housing stock mostly dates from the 1970s-90s building boom, so most homes here are still on their original drain lines. That's not old enough on its own to explain a clog. But it does mean root intrusion from established trees near the property line is a realistic cause for a main-line backup that keeps recurring, and it's worth checking with a camera before assuming another round of snaking will hold.

Signs You Need More Than a Plunger

Multiple Slow Drains

If more than one fixture drains slowly at the same time, the clog is likely in a shared line, not the individual fixture.

Gurgling Sounds

Air trapped behind a partial blockage often gurgles when a nearby fixture drains.

Recurring Clogs

A drain that clears and clogs again within weeks usually has a cause further down the line than a quick snake reaches.

Sewage Odor

A persistent sewer smell near a drain can mean a venting issue or a blockage trapping waste in the line.

FAQs

How do you clear a clog you can't reach with a plunger?

A drain snake (cable auger) breaks up or retrieves the clog directly. For a clog that keeps returning or covers a longer run of pipe, hydro jetting clears the full diameter of the line, including grease and root intrusion a snake can pass through but not remove.

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

It's safe for sound copper, PVC, and cast iron. A technician checks the pipe's condition first, since a severely corroded or already-cracked line can be damaged by high pressure.

Why does the same drain keep clogging?

A drain that clogs repeatedly, especially a main line, is often a sign of root intrusion or a partial blockage that a quick snaking pass never fully cleared. A camera inspection can confirm the actual cause.

Do you handle clogs for rental properties or HOA common lines?

Yes, we work directly with property managers and HOA boards on both individual-unit and shared-line drain issues.
