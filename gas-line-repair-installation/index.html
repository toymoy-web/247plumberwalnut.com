If You Smell Gas, Do This First

Don't operate switchesDon't turn any electrical switch, light, or appliance on or off near the smell, including your phone if you're inside.
Leave immediatelyGet everyone, including pets, out of the house right away. Don't stop to open windows or search for the source.
Call from outsideOnce you're safely away from the house, call SoCalGas's 24-hour gas leak line at 1-800-427-2200.

Common Gas Line Jobs

Beyond emergency situations, most gas line calls fall into a handful of common categories, each with a different scope and inspection requirement.

New Appliance Lines

Running a new line for a range, dryer, water heater, or outdoor grill and fire feature.

Line Repair

Fixing a line that's failed a pressure test or shows a leak during a home inspection.

Line Relocation

Moving a gas line during a kitchen or outdoor remodel.

Pressure Testing

Testing an existing system's integrity, often required before a real estate transaction or major appliance swap.

FAQs

What should I do if I smell gas in my house?

Leave the house immediately without operating any switches or appliances, then call SoCalGas's 24-hour gas leak line at 1-800-427-2200 from a safe distance. Once they confirm it's safe to go back inside, call us to diagnose and repair the actual issue.

Do I need a permit for a new gas line?

In most cases yes. We handle the permit and inspection process as part of the job rather than leaving it to you to arrange separately.

How long does a gas line installation take?

A straightforward appliance line, like for an outdoor grill, is often completed in a single visit; longer runs or line relocations can take longer depending on routing and inspection scheduling.

Can you test my existing gas line before I sell my house?

Yes, pressure testing an existing system is a common request ahead of a real estate transaction, and we can provide documentation of the results.
