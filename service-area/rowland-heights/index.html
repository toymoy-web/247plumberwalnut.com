Rowland Heights is an unincorporated Los Angeles County community, not a city, named after rancher John A. Rowland and sitting in and below the Puente Hills. It's one of the San Gabriel Valley's most recognizable commercial hubs, known regionally for the concentration of Asian shopping plazas along Colima Road and Nogales Street, alongside its residential neighborhoods.

What Makes Plumbing Different Here

Rowland Heights' identity as a commercial hub as much as a residential community means the plumbing work here covers a wider range than a typical single-use suburb, from restaurant kitchens to standard household calls.

Mixed Commercial & Residential

Rowland Heights' dense retail corridor means we handle both restaurant/retail plumbing and standard residential calls in the same community.

Puente Hills Terrain

Homes sitting below and within the Puente Hills sometimes deal with the same hillside drainage and slab considerations as other San Gabriel Valley foothill communities.

Shared Water Utility

Older Housing Stock
