Pomona is Walnut's neighbor to the east and the largest market in this part of the valley, with 151,713 residents at the 2020 census. Mt. San Antonio College sits on the Walnut side of the line, and Cal Poly Pomona lies partly within Pomona's city limits and partly in the neighboring unincorporated community of Walnut Islands, so the two cities share a busy campus corridor.

Pomona was incorporated in 1888, which makes it one of the oldest cities we cover. Its housing runs from historic districts such as Lincoln Park to residential areas such as Phillips Ranch, and that range is the main thing to understand about plumbing here. A house could carry pipes from any of several eras, so we check what is in the walls instead of guessing from the ZIP code.

What Pomona's Range of Housing Means for Plumbing

Historic District Homes

Older homes in areas like the Lincoln Park Historic District may have original cast iron drains, galvanized supply lines, or later partial repipes. Work on historic properties can also call for extra care around finishes and access.

Mixed Repair Histories

One block can hold original plumbing next to partial repipes and add-on bathrooms, so a leak does not always share a cause with the house next door.

Campus-Area Rentals

Near Cal Poly Pomona and Downtown, rentals and multi-unit buildings mean shared drain lines and landlord coordination. We document findings so owners and tenants see the same picture.

Fairplex and Commercial Areas

Event venues, warehouses, and downtown storefronts bring commercial needs such as backflow testing and large-diameter drain cleaning.

Services We Provide in Pomona

Drain cleaning, sewer line diagnostics, leak detection, and water heater replacement cover most Pomona calls. Repiping and commercial plumbing come up more in the older and busier parts of the city.
