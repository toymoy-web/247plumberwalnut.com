Tankless water heaters solve the "ran out of hot water" problem tank units have, but they come with their own maintenance needs, especially in a moderately hard water area like Walnut, CA, where mineral scale builds up on the unit's internal heat exchanger faster than it would in a soft-water region. Regular descaling is what keeps a tankless unit performing at its rated efficiency over its full lifespan.

Common Tankless Water Heater Issues

Inconsistent Temperature

Fluctuating hot water temperature often points to scale buildup restricting flow through the heat exchanger.

Error Codes

Most tankless units display a specific error code when something's wrong; we can diagnose the exact cause from the code rather than guessing.

No Hot Water at All

Can range from a simple ignition issue to a failed component, depending on what the unit's diagnostics show.

Reduced Flow Rate

Scale buildup inside the unit can restrict water flow even when the heating function itself still works.

Why Descaling Matters Here

Walnut Valley Water District's Annual Water Quality Report classifies local water as moderately hard, and most tankless manufacturers specifically recommend annual descaling in areas with hardness at or above that level to maintain the warranty and prevent premature heat exchanger failure. Skipping it doesn't cause immediate failure, but it does shorten the unit's effective lifespan and gradually reduces efficiency over several years of normal use, often without an obvious symptom until the reduction becomes significant.

Installation vs. Repair

New Installation

  • Includes gas line sizing check for gas-fired units
  • Venting installed to manufacturer specification
  • Often replaces a failing tank unit in the same visit

Repair & Descaling

  • Diagnostic based on the unit's error code or symptom
  • Descaling service to restore flow and efficiency
  • Component repair for ignition, sensor, or heat exchanger issues

FAQs

How often should a tankless water heater be descaled in Walnut, CA?

Most manufacturers recommend annual descaling in moderately hard water areas like this one, since mineral buildup on the heat exchanger reduces efficiency and shortens the unit's lifespan without it.

Can I switch from a tank to a tankless water heater?

In most cases yes, though gas line sizing and venting sometimes need to be upgraded to accommodate a tankless unit's higher demand, which we'll assess before quoting the project.

Why does my tankless water heater show inconsistent temperature?

That's most often caused by scale buildup restricting flow through the heat exchanger, though it can also point to a flow sensor or ignition issue depending on the specific pattern.

Do you work on all tankless brands?

Yes, we service all major tankless water heater brands, and can typically diagnose an issue from the unit's displayed error code before a technician even arrives.
