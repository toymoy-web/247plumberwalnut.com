How Hydro Jetting Differs From Snaking

Snaking (Cable Auger)

  • Breaks through or retrieves a localized clog
  • Fast and effective for most single-fixture issues
  • Can punch a hole through buildup without clearing the full pipe diameter
  • Root intrusion often returns within weeks

Hydro Jetting

  • High-pressure water clears the full interior diameter of the pipe
  • Removes grease, scale, and root intrusion, not just a channel through it
  • Better long-term solution for recurring clogs
  • Camera inspection confirms the pipe is a good candidate first

When Hydro Jetting Makes Sense

Not every drain issue calls for jetting; it's best suited to a specific set of situations where a standard snake simply isn't enough to solve the underlying problem long-term.

Recurring Clogs

A main line that clogs again within weeks of snaking is the clearest sign a full jetting pass is needed instead.

Root Intrusion

Established tree roots that have worked into pipe joints need more than a cable auger to actually clear out.

Grease Buildup

Restaurant and commercial kitchen lines especially benefit from periodic jetting to prevent grease accumulation.

Pre-Sale Maintenance

Some homeowners jet their main line proactively before a real estate transaction to avoid a surprise during inspection.

Is Hydro Jetting Safe for Older Pipes?

It's safe for sound copper, PVC, and cast iron. A technician checks the pipe's condition with a camera first, since a severely corroded or already-cracked line can be damaged by high pressure. That inspection is part of the visit, not an extra step you have to specifically request or pay for separately before we can tell you whether jetting is the right call for your specific line.

FAQs

How is hydro jetting different from regular drain snaking?

Snaking breaks through or retrieves a localized clog, while hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to clear the entire interior diameter of the pipe, which handles grease and root intrusion more thoroughly.

Is hydro jetting safe for my home's older pipes?

It's safe for sound copper, PVC, and cast iron. We check the pipe's condition with a camera first, since a severely corroded or cracked line could be damaged by the pressure.

How often should I have my main line jetted for maintenance?

It depends on your property's specific situation, especially nearby mature trees, but a periodic maintenance schedule can prevent a full backup for properties with a known root-intrusion history.

Can hydro jetting fix a collapsed pipe?

No, jetting cleans the interior of an otherwise structurally sound pipe; a collapsed or severely damaged section needs repair or replacement instead, which a camera inspection will confirm.
