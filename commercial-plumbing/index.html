Walnut's commercial corridor along Grand Ave and Lemon Ave, along with the office and retail space that fills out the rest of the city, runs on plumbing systems that a residential-only service call isn't set up to handle: grease traps, multi-fixture restrooms, backflow devices, and larger supply and waste lines than a typical home.

Commercial properties in California are subject to annual backflow testing requirements under the state's cross-connection control regulations (Title 17, California Code of Regulations), enforced locally by the water purveyor. It's an easy compliance item to lose track of, and we can test and certify a device as part of a service visit or as a standalone appointment.

What We Handle

The range of issues a commercial property runs into rarely matches a residential service call, since the fixture counts, pipe diameters, and code requirements are different from a single-family home. Below are the categories we see most often across Walnut's retail, office, and light-industrial properties.

Restroom Fixtures

Multi-fixture restroom repair and replacement for retail and office buildings, including ADA-compliant fixture installation.

Grease Trap Service

Cleaning and repair for restaurant and food-service grease interceptors.

Backflow Testing

Annual testing and certification for the backflow prevention devices California requires on commercial properties.

Water Heater Systems

Commercial-capacity water heater repair and replacement for offices and multi-tenant buildings.

Scheduling Around Business Hours

A burst line or backed-up restroom during business hours costs a commercial tenant more than the repair itself in lost operating time. We can schedule after-hours or early-morning work for jobs that would otherwise disrupt a retail floor or an office during the day, and coordinate directly with property managers who need work documented for a landlord or insurer.

Multi-tenant buildings add another layer: a shared main line issue can affect several businesses at once, which usually means coordinating access and timing across more than one tenant. We handle that scheduling directly with a property manager or building engineer rather than requiring each affected tenant to separately call in.

Commercial leases often put plumbing maintenance responsibility on the tenant for interior fixtures and on the landlord for the building's main lines, and it's not always clear which side of that line a given problem falls on. We can document a repair in a way that supports whichever conversation you need to have with a landlord, tenant, or insurer, without taking a position on the lease dispute itself. For multi-tenant buildings in Walnut's retail and office corridor, that documentation is often as useful as the repair itself when it comes time to sort out who's responsible for what.

Recurring commercial accounts, buildings with multiple plumbing systems that need periodic checks, are also worth setting up as a standing arrangement rather than a one-off call each time something breaks, since it gives us a record of the property's fixtures and past repairs to work from on the next visit. FAQs

Do you test and certify backflow devices?

Yes, we test and certify backflow prevention devices to meet the annual requirement most California water purveyors enforce on commercial properties.

Can you schedule work after business hours?

Yes, for jobs that would disrupt a retail floor or office during the day, we can schedule early-morning or after-hours work.

Do you work with property management companies?

Yes, we regularly coordinate directly with property managers and can document work for landlords, insurers, or tenant records.

Do you service restaurant grease traps?
